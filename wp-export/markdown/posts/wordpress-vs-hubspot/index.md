# WordPress vs. HubSpot: Which way to turn?

When it comes to building websites, using a content management system is clearly the way to go. There are open-source options like WordPress. And proprietary ones like HubSpot.

### WordPress: The Open-Source Powerhouse

Powering over 40% of all websites on the Internet, WordPress is the undisputed leader of the website content management world. This post is about WordPress.org, the free, open source platform. Not WordPress.com, which is a commercial, paid hosting service created by Automattic!

#### WordPress Pros:

**Extensive Plugin Library:** With over 70,000 plugins (both free and paid), WordPress makes it possible to add a wide range of functionalities to the site, from SEO to e-commerce to job applications to forms to carousels and sliders to security to many, many more. Quite often, free plugins work just fine. Think of a function and there's a plugin for that!

**Custom Themes:** Over 30,000 themes (free and paid) offer many design options, and one can also create custom themes to match unique needs. Again, free themes work well in many cases.

**Open Source:** As an open-source platform, WordPress's code is freely available for anyone to modify, offering lots of customization opportunities. Though, one should never edit WordPress core files!

**Vibrant Community:** A robust global community provides extensive resources, tutorials, forums, and regular updates. The community-driven nature ensures continuous improvement and resolutions to issues.

**Free Core Software:** WordPress itself is completely free to use. One only needs to pay for hosting, premium themes, and premium plugins if one chooses to use them.

**Scalable Hosting Options:** From budget-friendly shared hosting to private servers, WordPress can be hosted on various platforms to fit different budgets and needs.

**Full Ownership:** You own your website and its content, ensuring complete control over your digital presence. One can move the site to different hosting providers without significant hassles.

#### WordPress Cons:

**Regular Updates Required:** To keep the site secure, one needs to regularly update the WordPress core, theme, and plugins. Neglecting updates can expose the site to vulnerabilities.

**Security Management:** While WordPress is secure, its popularity and open-source nature makes it an attractive target for hackers. One needs to implement security measures and possibly use security plugins.

**Technical Knowledge Needed:** Although user-friendly, fully leveraging WordPress's capabilities does requires some technical expertise, particularly for advanced customization and troubleshooting.

### HubSpot: The Proprietary All-in-One Solution

With over 30% of the marketing automation market, HubSpot is the leader in this space. By combining its integrated marketing capabilities with content management, HubSpot offers a compelling proposition to those looking to build their websites.

#### HubSpot Pros:

**Intuitive Interface:** HubSpot offers a user-friendly interface that somewhat simplifies the process of building and managing websites and landing pages, making it accessible to users with minimal technical knowledge.

**Integrated Tools:** The HubSpot CMS is part of a larger suite of marketing, sales, and service tools, providing an integrated approach to managing digital marketing efforts.

**Managed Security:** This is a big one. HubSpot handles all security, updates, and maintenance, lowering the burden on users. This ensures the site remains secure and up to date without manual intervention.

**Reliable Support:** Dedicated customer support is available to assist with any issues or queries, providing peace of mind for users.

**SEO and Analytics:** HubSpot includes built-in SEO tools and analytics, allowing one to track and optimize site performance without needing additional plugins.

**Marketing Automation:** Advanced marketing features like email automation, lead nurturing, and CRM integration are readily available, enhancing marketing capabilities.

#### HubSpot Cons:

**Restricted Flexibility:** Compared to open-source platforms, HubSpot offers relatively fewer customization options. Users are often limited to the themes and templates provided by HubSpot. For example, the number of child themes is limited by the commercial license. Though, there is a marketplace with additional options, with both free and paid solutions.

**Dependence on Provider:** One’s ability to add custom features is limited by what HubSpot supports, which can restrict your site’s functionality. Again, there is a marketplace with additional options, with both free and paid solutions.

**Subscription Fees:** HubSpot is a premium service with subscription fees that can be significant, especially for small businesses or startups. Additional features often come at an extra cost.

**Scaling Expenses:** As needs grow, costs can escalate quickly, making HubSpot a potentially expensive solution over time.

**Vendor lock-in:** Moving the site from HubSpot to another platform can be complex and time-consuming. Content and design elements may not transfer seamlessly, leading to potential data loss and/or significant redevelopment costs.

**Dependence on HubSpot:** One relies entirely on HubSpot and their marketplace partners for updates, new features, and support. Any changes or discontinuations in service can significantly impact your website.

**Limited Community Resources:** Unlike WordPress, HubSpot does not have a large community of developers and users offering free resources, plugins, and themes. The HubSpot community is no match for what WordPress offers. This can limit your ability to find support and extend your site’s functionality.

### Conclusion

Choosing between WordPress and HubSpot depends on your specific needs, technical expertise, and budget. WordPress offers unmatched flexibility, control, and community support, making it ideal for those who require extensive customization and are willing to manage maintenance. HubSpot provides ease of use, integrated marketing tools, and managed security, appealing to users who prefer a comprehensive, hands-off solution despite higher costs and somewhat limited customization possibilities.
